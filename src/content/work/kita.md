# Kita management software
Legacy Windows App rebuilt and updated as web app, as part of a public / government services digitisation. I led the frontend of a 5-dev team (10 overall).
Full kita management:
- entering children's details, groups, schedule, attendance
- full state law compliance (Bavarian funding & vaccination rules), alerting on missing vaccinations
- WCAG AA accessibility
- keycloak authentication
- employee's details and attendance
- track meals
- invoices & book-keeping system
- accounting for state law subsidisations
- reporting & forecasting
